### IMPORTANT

The catalog must **not look like a traditional webshop with many small cards**.

Use a premium, spacious layout:

* large product images
* lots of white space
* large typography
* minimal text
* clean black/white design
* subtle SHUTERA blue accents
* smooth animations
* horizontal product navigation
* sticky category navigation
* elegant hover effects

**Every product must be clickable. Every product must have its own unique detail page.**

The product catalog should feel like a **premium technology brand experience**, not a generic online store.
