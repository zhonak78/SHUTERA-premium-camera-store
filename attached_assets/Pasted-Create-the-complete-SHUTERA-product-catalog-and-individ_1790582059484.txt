==================================================
PRODUCT PAGE BEHAVIOR
==================================================

Every product must have its own product detail page.

When the user clicks a product:
- open its product page
- automatically scroll to the very top
- do not preserve the previous page scroll position

Every product page must start from scroll position 0.

For products with variants:
- keep the user on the same product page
- do not reload the entire page
- dynamically change the image
- dynamically change the price
- dynamically change the specifications
- highlight the selected option

Example:

SHUTERA SD

[128 GB] [256 GB] [512 GB]

Clicking 256 GB must immediately display:
- 256 GB product image
- 35 € price
- 256 GB specifications

The same system must work for:
- memory cards
- SSDs
- batteries
- lenses
- chargers
- bags and other products with variants.

The product presentation should be clean, premium and spacious, with large product images, large typography and minimal unnecessary text.
